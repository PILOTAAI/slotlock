# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or role.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, give actionable feedback, and
keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and
the publication of private calendar or customer data are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by email to
[clever.eziogor@pylota.io](mailto:clever.eziogor@pylota.io), which reaches the maintainers listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md). If you would rather not use email, open a GitHub issue that asks
a maintainer to contact you and contains no details of the concern; a maintainer will arrange a
private conversation. A concern about a maintainer goes to another maintainer where there is one.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect
the community. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, community events,
and when representing the project elsewhere.
