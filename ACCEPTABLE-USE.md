# Acceptable Use Policy for hosted Slotlock

> **Draft — requires owner and legal review before the hosted service opens.**

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md) for hosted Slotlock, run by TREFT LTD
(trading as Pylota). It says what you may not do with the service, so that it stays safe and
available for everyone who shares it.

## Do not

- Break the law, or help anyone else to, with the service or the content you store in it.
- Store personal data you have no right to store, or more about anyone than your booking needs.
- Store special category data (health, religion, ethnicity, sexuality, biometrics and the other
  kinds UK GDPR Article 9 lists) or data about criminal convictions. A booking needs a time and a
  name, not a diagnosis.
- Try to read, change or infer another account's data, or get around tenant isolation, API key
  scopes or authentication.
- Get around the published limits: by opening more than one account for one person or
  organisation, by spreading requests across addresses or keys to escape a limit, or in any other
  way.
- Send traffic meant to slow or stop the service: floods, deliberately expensive requests such as
  recurrence rules written to expand without end, or load tests. Run Slotlock yourself to load test
  it.
- Probe or test the service's security except against your own account and as
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- Upload malware, or content meant to exploit the people or software that read it.
- Use the service to harass, track or monitor people without their knowledge.
- Resell or rent access to the service, or present it as your own service, without our written
  permission.
- Automate the creation of accounts or keys beyond what your own agents need.

## What we do

We may slow or refuse requests, revoke keys, suspend or erase an account, and report illegal
activity to the authorities. Where we can, we tell you first and give you a chance to stop. We act
at once, without notice, when the service or other people are at risk.

## Report abuse

Write to [abuse@pylota.io](mailto:abuse@pylota.io) with what happened, when (with the time zone),
and anything that identifies the account or requests involved, such as the API key prefix
(`slk_` and the characters after it), but never a whole key. We acknowledge reports within three
business days. Report a security vulnerability privately as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes,
not by email.
